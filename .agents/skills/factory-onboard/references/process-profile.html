Project factory profile

Keep this in the target repository's existing process document, or docs/FACTORY-PROFILE.md.

Describe the agreed operating process, not onboarding history. Omit irrelevant or empty sections.

  • Purpose and sources: the project's intended outcomes and authoritative context.
  • Responsibility and authority: who delegates, what the factory owns, who accepts the result,

and which decisions return to a person.

  • Work progression: local role/section and delivery-state mappings, transition owners,

waiting/resumption and closure rules. Runtime state is separate from delivery progress.

  • Rigor and understanding: consequences, checks, evidence and human checkpoints appropriate to the work.
  • Tools and boundaries: actual repository, harness, work/context destinations and permitted effects.
  • Selected components: the roles, skills and prompts this process needs, with their pinned source.
  • Unresolved decisions: what remains unknown and which dependent work must wait.
  • Context bindings: existing knowledge/search home, meetings and repository sources; source owner,

authorized scope/audience, discovered access and capabilities, freshness, persistence/retention,

known coverage gaps and locally agreed decision-retrieval readiness examples.
